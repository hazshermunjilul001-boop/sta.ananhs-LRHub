Learning Packets

In times of crisis or emergency situations—such as natural disasters, conflict, or public health emergencies—access to quality education is often disrupted. To ensure learning continuity and equity among all students nationwide, the provision of these Learning Packets serves as an essential intervention from the DepED Central Office. These materials are developed to be inclusive and accessible, enabling learners to continue their education at home or in temporary learning spaces, even without access to digital devices or reliable internet.

These packets reflect the national commitment to educational resilience and learner welfare. Prepared in alignment with the K to 12 curriculum, they are designed to support students across all grade levels (Grades 7-10) ensuring that no Filipino learner is left behind, especially during challenging times.
