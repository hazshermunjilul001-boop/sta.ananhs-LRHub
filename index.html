Welcome to the Digitalized Learning Resources Hub of Sta. Ana National High School!

In an age where digital platforms play a huge role in transforming education, effective management and easy access to learning resources are essential for empowering both educators and students.

Our Learning Management System is designed to support the Department of Education’s vision of a digital future by providing a streamlined platform to organize, share, and utilize educational materials efficiently.

This hub offers a comprehensive collection of resources, including PISA-like assessments to ensure our students are prepared for global academic standards, interactive learning materials, video lessons, and self-learning modules. Explore locally developed stories, illustrated story books, comics, and the renowned Project ML, Project CARE, Project Lunsaran materials, along with unified assessments across all learning areas.

This platform led by Welito I. Rosal, Principal III, Sta. Ana National High School, is continuously updated and maintained by the dedicated School Technical Working Group (STWG) composed of SANHS Head Teachers, ICT teachers and subject focal person.

Whether you’re an educator in search of structured course content or a student looking to enhance your knowledge, our platform provides the tools needed to enrich your learning journey. Join us in embracing the future of education—where technology meets opportunity, and learning knows no boundaries.
