PISA-like Materials

In response to the challenges after the recent standing of the Philippines on the Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) result, Sta. Ana National High School is committed to enhance the academic skills of the learners in Davao City and address the existing learning gaps. Our goal is to equip students with critical thinking, problem-solving, and analytical skills needed to succeed in global assessments and excel across key subjects such as reading, mathematics, and science.

This platform is designed to support educators, students, and learners alike by providing a comprehensive collection of interactive learning materials. This Digitalized Learning Resource Hub (LR) offers a user-friendly, interactive environment where students can access practice-oriented resources to strengthen their skills.
